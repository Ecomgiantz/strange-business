My Story

Filmmaker, Storyteller,
Visual Experimenter

I'm Joseph Leung, a media student at Singapore Polytechnic and an aspiring film director. I founded STRANGE BUSINESS as my personal creative brand — a space where unconventional storytelling meets visual experimentation.

My journey into filmmaking began with a simple observation: the most compelling stories aren't found in the dramatic or spectacular. They're woven into the fabric of everyday life — in the quiet moments between conversations, in the routines people repeat without thinking, in the faces of strangers on the last train home.

That insight became my creative philosophy: finding extraordinary stories in ordinary situations. Every project I take on is an attempt to shift perspective, to slow things down, and to make audiences see what was always there — just differently.

My work spans short films, documentaries, and visual experiments. I'm drawn to intimate, character-driven storytelling — the kind of filmmaking that sits with you long after the screen goes dark. I believe in the power of observation over spectacle, of silence over dialogue, of truth over production value.

Beyond filmmaking, I'm constantly experimenting — with photography, mixed-media art, sound design, and abstract visual techniques. The Creative Lab section of this portfolio is where those experiments live: raw, unfinished, and honest.

I'm currently pursuing my diploma at Singapore Polytechnic, where I'm developing my skills in cinematography, directing, post-production, and screenwriting. My ambition is to become a film director who tells the stories that matter — the ones hiding in plain sight.

"Ordinary is overrated — but it's also where the best stories hide. My job is to find them."

Joseph Leung — Creative Philosophy
